Sales Club Reward Budget Simulation

Monte Carlo experiment for the 14-team Sorbon Sales Club. Each scenario fixes the annual total Realized AOP, then randomly distributes that performance across months, teams, and members to estimate the cash needed at month-end, season-end, and year-end.
Settling point: 102% AOP
Fixed experiment settings
All monetary values use your MT notation.
MonthlyEach eligible team pays 50 MT to its best seller + β×50 MT to the manager.
SeasonalTeams are ranked inside each league. League A uses γ×rank factor; League B divides that reward by α.
AnnualIf total annual AOP ≥ 88%, fund League A champion 1,000 MT, League B champion 500 MT, and the 5,500 MT draw pool.
RandomizationMonthly/member outcomes vary randomly, but each run is rescaled so its annual total exactly matches the selected scenario.
Scenarios
The 88% gate is modeled explicitly because the requested scenarios bracket it at 87.9% and 88.1%. Change the threshold above if your final business rule differs.
